VEGAN MUM

Vegan Mum (Vegan Candle) is an %100 natural candle producer based in Istanbul. It was discontinued because of no market. I learned that the market research based on your relatives my not function for the real world. People simple didn't want to pay more for a natural candle. Also, the lack of motivation was another issue in the company. Lesson learned that entrepreneur and the team has to be obsessed with the problem they were trying to solve. In this scenario, Vegan Mum aimed to raise awareness for toxicity of paraffin waxes and offer the customer a healthy choice.

HD AERO SUPPLY

HD Aero Supply is a global supplier of commercial aircraft spare-parts, services, repairs, and overhaul support. My role in this company was a Co-Founder and Brand Designer. However, my bet against the pandemic didn't really work well for a broker company for the first 2 years. On the other hand, while starting the company we didn't have a long term strategy which caused de-motivations on my side as I was focused on the short term results. The company still generates income and acts as a small broker company, however it was time for me to look for other motivations and opportunities.

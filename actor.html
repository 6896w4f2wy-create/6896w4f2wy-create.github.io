Enes Yaman is a multidisciplinary visual artist and actor born on May 15th in Istanbul.

Initially trained in acting in Istanbul, he pursued his drama education in New York and conducted further research and studies between 2017 and 2019.

Having played in commercials, theatre productions and shorts, Enes, took part in international productions in the latest years.

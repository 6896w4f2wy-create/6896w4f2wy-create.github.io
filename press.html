2021 - Al-Araby Al-Jadeed - London

Three paintings of Actor and artist Enes Yaman were published at the art and literature section of the Al-Araby Al-Jadeed (The New Arab) daily newspaper in London. Al-Araby Al-Jadeed is the most prominent and widely distributed Arabic newspaper in the Arab World and in and is the only Arab newspaper that has an edition for Turkey and distributed on Turkish Airlines.

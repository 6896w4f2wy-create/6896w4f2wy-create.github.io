Statement

My painting series, "Mother and the Couch," initiated between 2021-2023, delves into the struggles faced by women in Turkish society, exploring the tension between comfort and confinement. It is a tribute to my own mother and all the women who persist in the face of adversity. Through this series, I hope to shed light on the resilience and determination of women in Turkey and around the world, inspiring viewers to appreciate their strength. Additionally, I have embarked on other projects that delve into human emotions, identity questions, and the search for meaning in life. From self-portraits to figurative sculpture and evocative photography, my work captures the intricacies of the human experience and invites viewers to engage in introspection and contemplation.

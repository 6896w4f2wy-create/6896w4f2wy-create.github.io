Enes Yaman

Born in 1996 in Istanbul, Turkey.

Initially trained in acting in Istanbul, he pursued his drama education in New York and conducted further research and studies between 2017 and 2019.

During taking part in short films, theatre productions and international film productions, he studied Photography (Anadolu University) and started a degree in Motion Pictures (Hochschule Darmstadt).

After many years in the world of drama, and visual arts Enes, started directing to tell stories.

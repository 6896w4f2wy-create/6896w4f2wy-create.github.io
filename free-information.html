Time has changed and there are no excuses to accomplish what you want to do anymore, except politics.. Education is free and it's online. Information is infinite. Over the last decade, I have been reading, researching and watching a lot of mentors, teachers and philosophers. I believe the less is more.

Any day in which I solve the same problem twice, I am not me.

For me all humans meant to do something different every day. The idea of repeating ourselves and "specializing" is a modern invention of specialization labor in the Industrial Revolution.

Therefore, I am sharing my essential suggestions for you below.

What you consume, you become.

- Enes Yaman

"You could say a track in going to a law school is a low risk track from one perspective, it may still be a very high risk track in the sense that you may not be doing something meaningful with your life"

- Peter Thiel, In a Lecture at Stanford for Startups, 2014
